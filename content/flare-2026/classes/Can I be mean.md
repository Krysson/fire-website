This class explores several simple but sadistic floor-based ties designed to cause (consensual) distress with our favorite medium, rope. We'll explore a variety of ties, ranging from subtle discomfort to deep intensity, as well tools, techniques, and conversations to build your perfectly sadistic scene. Whether you’ve just tied your first single column or are a seasoned rigger, these “quick and dirty” ties are just that–quick, simple, and sure to have your bottom sweating in seconds. Beyond technique, we'll also unpack through guided discussion about what it means to play with pain -- Why do we choose sadomasochism? How do we care for each other during and afterwards? Students are meant to leave the workshop with the tools to play sadistically, responsibly, and intentionally.

## Prerequisites

Rigger prerequisites: Any single column tie and knowledge of basic frictions (reverse tension, half hitch, munter hitch, hojo cuffs, yuki knot), knowledge of safety and risk in rope 

Bottom prerequisites: confident in-rope communication skills, knowledge of safety and risk in rope, confident ability to monitor and communicate signs of nerve compression

## What to Bring

At least four bundles of rope, a fabric blindfold like a bandana or tenegui, and a cutting tool
