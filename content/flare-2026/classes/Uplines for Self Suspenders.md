## Class Info

Self tiers like to do things the hard way. We often have tons of extra upline, limits on what we can reach, and far too many instances where we can't breathe. In this class, we will review tips and tricks on upline management, and hopefully provide some ideas on ways to make them cleaner, safer, and more fun. This fast paced class is a combination of different ideas and styles, and will go back and forth between teaching and practicing. This content is geared towards high-transition technically-minded self suspenders, who are looking for ways to level up their scenes.

## Prerequisites

Basic upline management and handling. Suspension worthy harnesses (recommended lower body and upper body)

## What to Bring

- Full Suspension Kit
