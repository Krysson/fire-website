## Class Info

Tired of rope suspensions that start with a long, awkward lull while the bottom waits for the harnesses to be tied? This hands-on class is all about transforming the beginning of your scenes into engaging experiences right from the first wrap. We'll explore how to use the harnesses and uplines you already know in more dynamic ways. We will cover quick ties to grab your bottoms attention, as well as creative ways to tie things already in your toolbox. This class is meant for riggers who want to make their scenes more interesting without needing anything new.

## Prerequisites

Prerequisites: Harnesses you feel comfortable tying for suspension and uplines.

## What to Bring

Suspension rope kit
