# Sydona & Faerie Bruises

Syd and Kitten both work full-time as kink professionals, regularly teaching, performing, and facilitating rope together. They're particularly interested in the intersection of technical structure, psychological dynamics, and embodied experience in rope, creating education around these topics that support autonomy, creativity, and critical thinking. Individually and together, they've presented in a range of local and international contexts, most notably at Anatomie (London), Devil Mask Studio (Los Angeles), KOR (Berlin), EspacioNos (Barcelona) and Studio Friction (Denver). Syd is resident educator at Temple and Hit Me Up—both in NYC—and together they perform in nightlife and performance spaces across NYC, Brooklyn, and beyond. Kitten recently toured with and taught alongside Tamandua at That Rope Place (Singapore), Cats Downstairs (Taipei), and E-flow (Shanghai). She runs a dungeon space in NYC and has facilitated kink events for many years. In addition to working as a rope model, she is also a professional dominatrix, which leads her to bring and share a unique perspective in her rope bottoming practice.  

Their ongoing education has included extensive study with international instructors, including Fede Kirigami and Marta Tenshiko, Tamandua, Ropunawa, Soptik and Sansei, Banana, Hua Hua, Murasaki Haruan, Hajime Kinoko, Yagami Ren, Naoko, Tamandua, and Naka Akira. For both of them, rope is a topic they never tire of discussing endlessly; they are grateful to be able to practice so frequently, so freely, so passionately, and to share this enthusiasm so extensively all around the world.
